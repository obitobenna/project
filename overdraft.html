Getting a little extra money, when you need it, should never be a problem. Our overdraft product ensures you're never blind-sided by those extra costs you never saw coming. From flexible repayment options to competitive interest rates, we're here to support you every step of the way.

How to get a Pecan Overdraft

When you open and use your Pecan bank account frequently, you may be offered an overdraft automatically or you can apply for an overdraft easily in-app and we will check with a credit agency to ensure that the overdraft is affordable and manageable for you as at the period of your application.
