FAQs

Start by downloading our free mobile app from the App Store or Google Play Store. Once it's installed, all you need to open an online money account is:

  • An email address and phone number
  • An ID document, like your voter's card and NIMC slip (NIN)

We'll ask you for a short video selfie in the app to confirm your identity and protect you from fraud. Once that's done, all that's left is to start using your app to spend, stash and receive money.

No, you don't need proof of address to create your Pecan account. We only require your email address, phone number, and a valid ID document to get started.

You can start using your Pecan account immediately after verification. The entire sign-up process takes less than 5 minutes, and once approved, you'll have instant access to all features including transfers, bill payments, and your virtual debit card.

You can make deposits into your Pecan savings account through several convenient methods:

  • Bank transfers from other accounts
  • Card funding through the app
  • Direct deposits from employers
  • Cash deposits at designated partner locations

All deposits are processed instantly and reflect in your account immediately.
